# "Why you?" (free-text application fields, ~2,000 chars)

Three paragraphs, in this order. Save as `applications/<company>/why-you.txt`, count with `wc -c`.

1. **The wall.** Identity line, then the things they have shipped or delivered as concrete nouns, one number each. End on why they do it, in their words.
2. **The day job.** Three proof points from profile.json, max.
3. **Belief with cover.** The honest gap first ("I'm not a <x>"), then how they learn ("I learn a domain by deploying into it"), then the belief tied to the company's own words, then the wanting, once, at the end.

Rules: verbs do the work (pushed, retired, stepped in, delivered). No "passionate," "excited," "leverage." One hard number per item. Short declaratives at paragraph ends. Numbers must match anything public the reader can click.
